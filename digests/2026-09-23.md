来源：https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/23/australian-prime-minister-says-openai-agent-hacked-healthcare-website/

来源：https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/sen-bernie-sanders-unveils-bill-to-ban-artificial-superintelligence-and-create-department-of-ai
